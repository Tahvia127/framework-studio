Questions

Before you ask

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes, on Business and Premium builds. You get a CMS and, on Premium, a training session where we walk through it together. The point is that you stop needing me for a menu change.

Do I own the site when it's finished?

Completely. Your domain, your hosting account, your code. Nothing is locked to me and there is no proprietary platform you have to keep paying for.

What if I need more pages than the package includes?

Extra pages are quoted before we start, so the price you agree is the price you pay. If the scope genuinely changes mid-project we agree the cost in writing before I build it.

Do you work with nonprofits?

Often. Nonprofit projects are quoted at the standard rate with a discount applied on the invoice, so both of us know what the work was actually worth.

How fast can you start?

The deposit holds your place in the schedule. Depending on the queue that is usually two to four weeks out. Rush delivery is available at a 25% premium.

What do you need from me?

Copy, images and logo files if you have them, and one decision-maker who can approve work. Projects stall on unclear approval far more often than on design.

Let’s talk about your site

Tell me what you're building and roughly what you want to spend. If I'm not the right fit I'll say so and point you somewhere better.
